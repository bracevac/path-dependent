\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
\mathplain
\suppressfloats[t]

Capture prediction for DOT follows, as type safety did in \Rone, from a translation into \FCdot in which every use of subtyping is explicit evidence and every binder declares the use set of its body. Beyond report~1 the argument needs two results. The first is a theorem about closed capture evidence: it relates only capture sets whose leaves are included (Theorem~\ref{thm:mini-cap} in the miniature, \lean{cap\_canon} in the trees). The second is the use-set half of preservation (Lemma~\ref{lem:mini-step}, \lean{step\_uses}), whose cases discharge the evidence that the typing of each redex carries. From the two, the leaves of the use set of a run never grow (Theorem~\ref{thm:mini-pred}), and a run never inspects a variable with a leaf outside them (Theorem~\ref{thm:mini-effect}). The proof uses no logical relation, no tight typing and no pushback measure.

\paragraph{What the trees share.}
The three trees share the five kinds of theorem of report~1 and the theorems about capture evidence. They differ in further theorems, as Table~\ref{tab:matrix} shows. The compiler's scoped reading of $\cany$ (\treeCC) and capability classifiers (\treeCL) keep the proof. They change what a leaf is. \treeCC adds \lean{level\_inversion}, a theorem about member-free evidence that needs no store, and \treeCL adds \lean{kind\_canon}, a theorem about kinding evidence. The files differ most in substitution (Section~\ref{sec:levels-run}). \lean{TypingSubst.lean} has 631 lines in \treeK and 2{,}986 in \treeCC, while \lean{CanonicalForms.lean} has 784 and 832. Table~\ref{tab:reading} lists, for each kind of evidence, the fact that it implies.

\paragraph{Evidence from a compiler.}
Report~1 ends with the remark that a compiler could emit evidence for the subsumption steps it takes, as GHC emits coercions, and have it checked. Capture checking is an instance. A subcapturing question of the compiler has evidence that erases, a checker decides the evidence, and a theorem says what checked evidence means. The front ends of Section~\ref{sec:frontends} run this on a model of the compiler's typer. The guarantee is narrower than a reader may hope. It concerns the variable that a step inspects, and it holds for the use set that a term declares. No theorem relates the model to the compiler.
