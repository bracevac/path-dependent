\section{Mechanization}\label{sec:mech}
\mathplain

Every Lean statement cited here is a declaration in one of the three trees, and the report names the tree. The development uses no library, and its toolchain is \lean{leanprover/lean4:v4.29.1}.

\subsection{Layout and lineage}\label{sec:mech-trees}

All paths are relative to \lean{lean/Coercions}. The three trees are \lean{Captures/}, \lean{CapturesCC/} and \lean{Classifiers/}. Each has the directories of the development of \Rone, under a namespace of its own. \lean{DotMNF/} is the source, \lean{FCdot/} the target, \lean{DotToFCdot/} the translation and \lean{Frontend/} the pipeline of Section~\ref{sec:frontends}. \treeCL also has \lean{Cls/}, the classifier tree and the kinds. Canonical forms, prediction, level inversion and root inversion are in \lean{CanonicalForms}, \lean{Prediction}, \lean{LevelInversion} and \lean{Consistency} of \lean{FCdot/}. The translation and source theorems are in \lean{TermsTyped}, \lean{EvidenceTyped}, \lean{Prediction} and \lean{Safety} of \lean{DotToFCdot/}, and the pipeline theorems in \lean{Frontend/Pipeline.lean}. A tree is a copy of its predecessor, and the three do not import one another. The file \lean{BASE} of each tree names the commit of the copy. \treeK is a copy of the vanilla tree, report~1's development without capture sets, at \lean{83079d4}, before PR~\#56. \treeCC is a copy of \treeK at \lean{a4df821}, and \treeCL a copy of \treeCC at \lean{90fab2a}.

This report displays no normalizer case, because the capture half is never normalized. The capture trees keep the forms of report~1 from before PR~\#56, which differ from its Figure~\ref{R1-fig:forms} in four ways. First, a head form $\form{eqv}\,\varphi$ holds closed equality evidence $\varphi$, which report~1 folds into $\form{id}$. Second, object forms and $\form{into}$ forms share one list of entries, in which a routed entry $\form{thru}\,F\,E$ may stand anywhere. Report~1 routes free entries only. Third, there is no split into local and free entries, and so no local entry $\form{trans}$ or $\form{copy}$. Fourth, the evidence lacks report~1's composition template $\ev{leTrans}$, which report~1's translation never produces. The capture trees add three things. The head form $\form{boxed}\,d$ holds closed evidence $d$ between the boxed types. In an object form the entries $\form{leC}$ and $\form{eqC}$ keep a capture template, while the capture forms of a view that Section~\ref{sec:target-canon} types under the same names carry no data. The capture cases were not ported to report~1's forms.

The Lean has the capture bounds $\cstar$, $\scopeb$, $\mathsf{upper}\,C$ and $\instb{C}$, and \treeCL a fifth, $\clsb{k}$. The report omits $\mathsf{upper}\,C$, which no rule or step constructs, though \rn{Kd-CVar} of \treeCL accepts it. \rn{S-Cap} accepts every bound in \treeK and every bound but $\scopeb$ in \treeCC and \treeCL, so the theorems also hold over stores with any such bound.

\subsection{Axioms and checks}\label{sec:mech-checks}

The libraries build with \lean{lake build} \lean{Captures} \lean{CapturesCC} \lean{Classifiers} \lean{CapturesFrontend} \lean{CapturesCCFrontend} \lean{ClassifiersFrontend} from the root of the repository. Each front-end library also imports two modules of report~1's front end, \lean{Frontend/Fuel.lean} and \lean{Frontend/Reason.lean}. The six libraries were built without error from a scratch copy of the trees at \lean{de9b596}, together with the \lean{Examples} modules of the three \lean{FCdot} directories. A search of the three trees finds no \lean{sorry}, no \lean{axiom}, no \lean{native\_decide} and no \lean{partial}, \lean{unsafe} or \lean{opaque} definition. On the same trees, \lean{Lean.collectAxioms} finds at most \lean{propext} and \lean{Quot.sound} below every declaration of the trees and their front ends whose name this report cites. The machine of \FCdot is also a function, \lean{fcStep?}, proved to agree with the step relation, but no compiled program runs on it.

In the status marks of Section~\ref{sec:intro}, a decided example runs a checker in the kernel, as \lean{C1\_typed} is \lean{checkValue\_sound (by decide +kernel)}, or states its verdict, as \lean{C7\_rejected} does (Section~\ref{sec:source-open}). Built by hand are runs written with the step constructors (\lean{C6\_run}), resolution facts proved by \lean{simp} on the clause lemmas of the well-founded \lean{Ctx.caps}, and translated examples (Section~\ref{sec:source-trans}).

\begin{enumerate}
\item It has named binders, and the Lean is de Bruijn. Store extension carries a renaming $\pi$, as \lean{Store.Ext} does. A Lean statement that concludes a store extension, such as capture prediction, is existential in $\pi$, and one that assumes it, such as \lean{Store.Ext.roots}, holds for every $\pi$. Named binders make $\pi$ the identity.
\item It has no fuel. The Lean's resolution takes fuel for capture names, which the miniature lacks. The roots of the use set of $\mathit{prog}_2$ are the same at every fuel (\lean{C6\_prog2\_caps}, built by hand).
\item Its atoms are variables. The Lean's atoms have wrappers (\lean{cast}, \lean{recap}, \lean{foldSelf}, \lean{unfoldSelf}, \lean{both}). \rn{E-App} is \lean{appVar}, and the Lean has two more application steps, \rn{E-AppCastRefl} and \rn{E-AppCast}, which read the chain of casts.
\item It writes store binders as $x : T$. The Lean binds them transparently with empty witnesses (\lean{C6Ctx}) and checks $v_{c1}$ under opaque binders (\lean{C1Ctx}). Let binders and parameters are opaque.
\item Theorem~\ref{thm:mini-cap} holds in every context. \lean{cap\_canon} in \treeK assumes a typed store, as a section variable, and covers all evidence. The statement with no store, for member-free evidence, is \lean{root\_inversion}, which exists in \treeCC only.
\item Theorem~\ref{thm:mini-pres} is proved on paper. Its counterpart \lean{preservation'} renames the type, and the renaming is not tied to \lean{Store.Ext}.
\item The run of $\mathit{prog}_2$ in Example~\ref{ex:mini-prog2} is on paper. The Lean states \lean{C6\_safe} for every run and writes none out.
\end{enumerate}
