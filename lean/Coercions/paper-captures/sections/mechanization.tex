\section{Mechanization}\label{sec:mech}
\mathplain

Every Lean statement that this report cites is a declaration in one of the three trees, and the report names the tree. The development has no library dependencies, so there is no Mathlib, and the toolchain is \lean{leanprover/lean4:v4.29.1}.

\subsection{Layout and lineage}\label{sec:mech-trees}

All paths are relative to \lean{lean/Coercions}. The three trees are \lean{Captures/}, \lean{CapturesCC/} and \lean{Classifiers/}. Each has the directories of the development of \Rone, under a namespace of its own. \lean{DotMNF/} is the source, \lean{FCdot/} the target, \lean{DotToFCdot/} the translation and \lean{Frontend/} the pipeline of Section~\ref{sec:frontends}. \treeCL also has \lean{Cls/}, the classifier tree and the kinds. A tree is a copy of its predecessor, and the three do not import one another. The file \lean{BASE} of each tree names the commit of the copy. \treeK is a copy of the vanilla line at \lean{83079d4}, which is the development of report~1 before PR~\#56. \treeCC is a copy of \treeK at \lean{a4df821}, and \treeCL a copy of \treeCC at \lean{90fab2a}.

PR~\#56 simplified the forms of report~1 after the copy of \treeK was taken. So the forms of the capture trees differ from those of report~1's Figure~\ref{R1-fig:forms} in four ways. First, a head form $\form{eqv}\,\varphi$ holds closed equality evidence $\varphi$ between the endpoints. Report~1 folds it into $\form{id}$. The capture trees keep both, so \rn{E-AppCastRefl} accepts a chain of either form (Table~\ref{tab:target-steps}). Second, object forms and $\form{into}$ forms share one list of entries. In it a routed entry $\form{thru}\,F\,E$ may stand anywhere. Its head form $F$ reaches another object type from the source, and its entry $E$ proves the target proposition there. Report~1 routes free entries only. Third, there is no split into local and free entries, and so no local entry $\form{trans}$ or $\form{copy}$. Fourth, the evidence lacks report~1's composition template $\ev{leTrans}$, which report~1's own translation never produces. The capture trees add three things. The head form $\form{boxed}\,d$ holds closed evidence $d$ between the boxed types. The entries $\form{leC}$ and $\form{eqC}$ keep a capture template as it is. A capture entry of a view carries no data. This report displays no normalizer case, because the capture half is never normalized. The capture cases were not ported to the forms of report~1.

Fixes made to \treeK after the copies were not all ported, and later strengthenings of \treeCC are not in \treeCL. Table~\ref{tab:matrix} lists the cited theorems whose existence or statement differs between the trees.

\subsection{Axioms and checks}\label{sec:mech-checks}

The libraries build with \lean{lake build Captures CapturesCC Classifiers CapturesFrontend CapturesCCFrontend ClassifiersFrontend} from the root of the repository. Each front-end library imports its own calculus and two modules of report~1's front end, \lean{Frontend/Fuel.lean}, the tank of Section~\ref{sec:fe-reject}, and \lean{Frontend/Reason.lean}, the reasons for a rejection. Table~\ref{tab:sizes} counts their 1{,}049 lines in the vanilla row only. The six libraries were built without error from a scratch copy of the trees at \lean{de9b596}, together with the \lean{Examples} modules of the three \lean{FCdot} directories. A search of the three trees finds no \lean{sorry}, no \lean{axiom}, no \lean{native\_decide} and no \lean{partial}, \lean{unsafe} or \lean{opaque} definition.

The command \lean{\#print axioms} finds at most \lean{propext} and \lean{Quot.sound} on \lean{capture\_prediction}, \lean{effect\_safety} and \lean{dot\_effect\_safety} in every tree, on eight more cited theorems and six cited examples, and on more than seventy theorems of the front ends.

Examples are checked in four ways. A kernel decision runs a checker. It applies the checker's soundness theorem to an accepted example, as in \lean{C1\_typed}, which is \lean{checkValue\_sound (by decide +kernel)}, and states the verdict \lean{= false} for a refused one, as in \lean{C6\_rejected}. A run is built by hand from the step constructors, as in \lean{C6\_run}. A resolution fact is proved by \lean{simp} on the clause lemmas, because \lean{Ctx.caps} is defined by well-founded recursion and its equations are the clause lemmas. A translated example is a theorem about a derivation and not a computation (Section~\ref{sec:source-trans}).

\begin{enumerate}
\item It has named binders, and the Lean is de Bruijn. Store extension, capture prediction and effect safety carry a renaming $\pi$, as \lean{Store.Ext} does, and are existential in it. With names $\pi$ is the identity, and the miniature drops it.
\item It has no fuel. The Lean's resolution takes fuel for capture names, which the miniature does not have. \lean{C6\_prog2\_caps} holds at every fuel.
\item Its atoms are variables. The Lean's atoms have wrappers (\lean{cast}, \lean{recap}, \lean{foldSelf}, \lean{unfoldSelf}, \lean{both}). \rn{E-App} is \lean{appVar}, and the Lean has two more application steps, \rn{E-AppCastRefl} and \rn{E-AppCast}, which read the chain of casts.
\item It writes store binders as $x : T$. The Lean binds them transparently with empty witnesses (\lean{C6Ctx}) and checks $v_{c1}$ in a context whose binders are opaque (\lean{C1Ctx}). Let binders and parameters are opaque in the Lean.
\item Theorem~\ref{thm:mini-cap} holds in every context. \lean{cap\_canon} in \treeK assumes a typed store, as a section variable, and covers all evidence. The statement with no store, for member-free evidence, is \lean{root\_inversion}, which exists in \treeCC only.
\item Theorem~\ref{thm:mini-pres} is proved on paper. Its counterpart \lean{preservation'} renames the type, and the renaming is not tied to \lean{Store.Ext}.
\item The run of $\mathit{prog}_2$ in Example~\ref{ex:mini-prog2} is on paper. The Lean states \lean{C6\_safe} for every run of $\mathit{prog}_2$ and writes none out.
\end{enumerate}
