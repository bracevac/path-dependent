Every soundness proof for Dependent Object Types (DOT) works around subsumption. Under a binder of type $\dtyp{A}{\top}{\bot}$ any two types are related, so the proofs argue that such a binder is never realized, by possible types, by tight and invertible typing, by pushing transitivity back through derivations, or by a semantic model, and each argument is tied to its rule set.

We translate DOT typing derivations into \FCdot, a calculus with no subsumption in which every use of subtyping, of type equality, and of field presence is a proof term that erases to nothing. Types mention terms only through the names of a binder's block, object types are telescopes of propositions over a self block, and coercions between them are morphisms of templates. Type safety for \FCdot is store typing plus one theorem, that closed evidence over a typed store normalizes to a head form, proven by structural induction. Evidence through bad bounds is well formed but inconsistent, and the consistency of every reachable store is a corollary.

The translation is total on derivations, homomorphic on types, type preserving, and coherent, and the safety of DOT in monadic normal form follows without any metatheory of its own. Everything is mechanized in Lean~4 without library dependencies and depends on propositional extensionality and quotient soundness only. The checker is decidable and complete.
