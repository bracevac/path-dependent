Every soundness proof for Dependent Object Types (DOT) has to work around subsumption. Under a binder of type $\dtyp{A}{\top}{\bot}$ any two types are related, so the proofs argue that such a binder is never realized: by possible types, by tight and invertible typing, by pushing transitivity back through derivations, or by a semantic model. Each argument is tied to the rule set it was made for.

We take a different route. We translate DOT typing derivations into \FCdot, a calculus with no subsumption in which every use of subtyping, of type equality, and of field presence is a proof term that erases to nothing. A type of \FCdot mentions terms only through the names of a binder's block, an object type is a telescope of propositions over a self block, and evidence between object types is a morphism of templates. Type safety for \FCdot is store typing plus one theorem: over a typed store, closed evidence normalizes to a head form. The proof is a structural induction on the typing derivations of evidence, with no step index and no semantic model. Evidence obtained through bad bounds is well formed but inconsistent, and the consistency of every reachable store is a corollary rather than a hypothesis.

The translation is a total function on derivations, homomorphic on types, type preserving, and coherent: the erasure of the translated term is the erasure of the source term. Type safety of the source, DOT in monadic normal form with variable paths, follows without any metatheory of its own. Everything is mechanized in Lean~4 without library dependencies. The main theorems depend on propositional extensionality and quotient soundness, and only progress, the backward erasure simulation, and their consequences take a classical case split. The checker is decidable and complete, and the evidence of every example is checked by the kernel.
